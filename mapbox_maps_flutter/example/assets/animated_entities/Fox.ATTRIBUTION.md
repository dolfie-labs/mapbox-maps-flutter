# Fox

This is the unchanged Fox GLB from Khronos glTF Sample Assets, pinned to commit
`edc7c9e67c639d230715049ee31f9a96a6babbbe`. Its original clip names are
`Survey` (0), `Walk` (1), and `Run` (2). Movey maps Survey to its Idle state;
the source animation has not been renamed or edited.
